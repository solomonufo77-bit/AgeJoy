AgeJoy Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

AgeJoy is an application designed to provide age calculation, birthday reminders, birthday wishes, prayers, inspirational quotes, zodiac information, favorites, and related features.

1. Information We Handle

AgeJoy may use information that you enter into the application, such as a name and date of birth, to provide app features. Information stored locally on your device is intended to remain on your device unless you choose to share it.

2. Birthday Reminders

If you enable birthday reminders, AgeJoy may request permission to send notifications on your Android device. Birthday reminder information may be stored locally on your device so that reminders can be scheduled.

3. Local Storage

AgeJoy may use local device storage to remember preferences, favorites, birthday information, and other application settings. This information is intended to remain on your device.

4. Notifications

Notifications are optional. You can allow or deny notification permission through your Android device settings. If notification permission is denied, birthday reminder notifications may not work.

5. Sharing

AgeJoy may allow you to share birthday wishes or other content using sharing features provided by your device. When you choose to share content, the information is transferred through the application or service you select.

6. Third-Party Services

AgeJoy does not intentionally sell your personal information. The application may use Android platform services and other technical services required for certain features. Those services may have their own privacy policies.

7. Children's Privacy

AgeJoy does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Users should avoid entering unnecessary sensitive personal information into the application.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Choices

You can choose whether to use optional features such as birthday reminders and notifications. You may also clear locally stored application data through your Android device settings.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when AgeJoy's features or services change. Any updated version will be published through the AgeJoy privacy-policy webpage.

11. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the AgeJoy app administrator through the contact information provided with the application.
