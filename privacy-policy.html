Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 9, 2026

1. What AgeJoy does

AgeJoy is an age and celebration companion. It provides age calculations, birthday countdowns and reminders, life milestones, zodiac and birthday facts, wishes, prayers, daily motivation and birthday-card creation.

2. Data handled on your device

Names and dates that you enter for birthday reminders are stored locally on your device so the reminder list can work. Age calculations and generated messages are processed in the app. AgeJoy does not require an account and the current build has no server database for these entries.

3. Notifications

Notifications are optional. If you enable them, the operating system uses notification permission to deliver app reminders or messages. AgeJoy does not need phone, contacts, Bluetooth, NFC, Wi-Fi, storage, or overlay permissions for its core features.

4. Sharing

If you tap Share, you choose the destination app using the device share mechanism. The selected third-party service handles anything you send through it under its own privacy policy. AgeJoy does not choose the recipient or send messages automatically.

5. Advertising and analytics

The current AgeJoy build does not include an advertising SDK, analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, or other third-party data-collection SDK. If an advertising or analytics service is added in a future release, this policy and the Google Play Data Safety disclosure will be updated to describe the service and its data practices before that release is published.

6. Collection and sharing

Based on the current AgeJoy code and dependencies, AgeJoy does not collect or share user-provided birthday names or dates with AgeJoy servers or third-party SDKs. Locally stored values remain on the device unless you intentionally share content through another app.

7. Retention and deletion

Birthday reminder entries remain locally on your device until you delete them in AgeJoy or clear the app's local data/uninstall the app. There is no AgeJoy account and therefore no AgeJoy account data to delete.

8. Children

AgeJoy is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. The current app does not require account registration or submission of personal information to AgeJoy.

9. Security

The current app keeps core user-entered birthday data locally. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Contact

Privacy questions can be submitted through the developer contact mechanism shown on the AgeJoy Google Play listing. The AgeJoy app name is used in the Play listing and in this policy.

11. Changes

We will update this page when AgeJoy's data practices change and revise the effective date.
