AgeJoy Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

AgeJoy is designed to provide age calculation, birthday reminders, birthday-related content, wishes, prayers, inspirational quotes, zodiac information, favorites, and related features.

Information We Handle

AgeJoy may use information that you enter into the app, such as a name and date of birth, to provide its features. Where information is stored locally on your device, it remains on your device unless you choose to share it elsewhere.

Birthday Reminders

If you enable birthday reminders, AgeJoy may request permission from your Android device to send notifications. Reminder information may be stored locally on your device so the app can schedule and display reminders.

Local Storage

AgeJoy may use local device storage to remember preferences, favorites, and other app settings. This information is intended to remain on your device.

Notifications

Notifications are optional. You can allow or deny notification permission through your Android device settings. If notification permission is denied, birthday reminder notifications may not work.

Third-Party Services

AgeJoy may use third-party services or platform features required for app operation, analytics, hosting, advertising, or other functionality if those services are added to the application. Their own privacy policies may apply.

Children's Privacy

AgeJoy is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. Users should avoid entering unnecessary sensitive personal information into the app.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information used by the app. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

You can choose whether to use optional features such as birthday reminders and notifications. You can also clear locally stored app data through your Android device settings.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when AgeJoy's features or services change. The updated version will be published through the app or its associated website.

Contact

For privacy questions or concerns about AgeJoy, please contact the AgeJoy app administrator through the contact information provided with the application.
